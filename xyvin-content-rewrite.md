## Words To Avoid Or Use Carefully

- Avoid: transformed, innovative solutions, game-changer, top-notch, remarkable, cutting-edge, seamless, digital transformation, unlock, elevate.
- Use instead: built, improved, connected, automated, simplified, launched, supported, reduced manual work, brought data together, made it easier for the team.

## Best Next Content To Collect

- For About page: one or two founder details that only Xyvin could say.
- For blog: one client lesson or project example per article.
